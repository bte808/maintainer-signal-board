# Code of Conduct

Maintainer Signal Board welcomes contributors who keep discussion practical, respectful, and focused on maintainable work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate of maintainers, contributors, and reporters.
- Critique code, docs, and decisions without personal attacks.
- Share synthetic examples when public data would expose private people, repositories, or organizations.
- Keep security and privacy reports careful, factual, and coordinated.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory remarks.
- Publishing private data, secrets, tokens, or non-consensual personal information.
- Misrepresenting the project as affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI or any other organization.
- Spam, repeated off-topic promotion, or pressure to accept unrelated scope.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; restrict participation; or block users when behavior harms the project or community. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented when practical, and focused on restoring a useful maintainer workflow.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the repository maintainer contact channel. If the concern includes a security or privacy issue, follow `SECURITY.md` and avoid posting sensitive details in a public issue.
